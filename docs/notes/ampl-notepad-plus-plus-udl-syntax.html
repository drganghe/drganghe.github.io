AMPL Notepad++ UDL Syntax

Author

Gang He

Published

May 1, 2014

I use AMPL for my modeling stuff, and I like to use text editor to deal with coding. Notepad++ can be a good alternative to BBEdit but AMPL is not included in the UDL list yet. So you have to make your own, as I did. To save your time, you may copy the following text to your Notepad++ and save as ampl.xml file, or download from here, then import from “Language -> Define your language -> Import”.

Well, this is not a complete list, but fair enough, let me know if you have one. Enjoy!
